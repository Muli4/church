Our Mission

To preach the Gospel of Jesus Christ, nurture believers spiritually, and serve the community with love, compassion, and integrity.

Our Vision

To be a Christ-centered church transforming lives through worship, discipleship, evangelism, and community service.

Who We Are

You Are Welcome Here

AIC Kongowea is a congregation under the African Inland Church dedicated to spreading the Gospel and impacting lives through biblical teaching, prayer, worship, and community outreach.

Founded to serve the spiritual needs of the Kongowea community, our church has continued to grow as a center of faith, hope, and transformation. Through the years, we have remained committed to uplifting families, mentoring youth, and supporting the local community.
