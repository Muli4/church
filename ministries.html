Find Your Ministry

Whether you're a child, youth, adult, or senior — there's a place for you at AIC Kongowea

Youth Ministry — Empowered for Christ

Our Youth Ministry is a vibrant community of young people ages 13-25 who are passionate about knowing God and making Him known. We meet weekly for worship, Bible study, mentorship, and fun activities designed to build character and deepen faith.

Saturdays: 3:00 PM - 5:00 PM
Youth Hall, Ground Floor
Leader: Pastor David Omondi
Join Youth Ministry

Women Ministry — Women of Purpose

The Women's Ministry exists to encourage women to grow in faith, leadership, and service. Through conferences, prayer meetings, mentorship circles, and outreach programs, we equip women to fulfill their God-given potential in the church and community.

Wednesdays: 10:00 AM - 12:00 PM
Fellowship Hall
Leader: Mama Ruth Akinyi
Join Women Ministry

Men Fellowship — Warriors of Faith

Men's Fellowship provides a space for men to connect, grow spiritually, and support one another. We focus on building strong Christian character, leadership development, accountability, and active participation in church and family life.

Saturdays: 7:00 AM - 8:30 AM
Main Sanctuary
Leader: Brother Joseph Kimathi
Join Men Fellowship

Children Ministry — Little Disciples

We nurture children ages 3-12 with age-appropriate Bible lessons, songs, crafts, and activities. Our dedicated team of teachers helps children build a strong biblical foundation, learn memory verses, and develop a personal relationship with Jesus.

Sundays: 10:30 AM - 12:00 PM
Children's Church Wing
Leader: Sister Mercy Njeri
Join Children Ministry

Worship Ministry — Leading in Praise

The Worship Ministry leads the congregation into God's presence through spirit-filled praise and worship. We welcome singers, musicians, sound technicians, and media operators who are passionate about using their gifts to glorify God.

Wednesdays: 6:00 PM - 8:00 PM (Rehearsal)
Main Sanctuary
Leader: Brother Caleb Otieno
Join Worship Ministry

Media Ministry — Digital Discipleship

The Media Ministry shares the gospel through technology, social media, live streaming, photography, videography, and graphic design. We ensure the message reaches beyond the church walls to a global audience.

Sundays & Special Events
Media Booth / Virtual
Leader: Brother Ian Mwangi
Join Media Ministry
